WHY STATION ZERO

A small team of careful minds, facing a hard problem.

In the 1940s, a quiet group of mathematicians and engineers at Bletchley Park’s Station X took on an adversary more capable than any single person. Among them was Alan Turing, whose work went on to found computer science and artificial intelligence.

Station Zero carries that spirit to Mumbai. Zero is India’s gift to mathematics, and the slashed zero is the engineer’s mark. We study how to understand, test and secure AI systems before they outpace our ability to oversee them.

THE HUTS

Six workstreams, one mission

HUT 1

ALIGNMENT

Keeping AI goals and values on course as systems grow more capable.

HUT 2

RED TEAM

Attacking our own systems first: jailbreaks, prompt injection and adversarial testing.

HUT 3

INTERPRETABILITY

Seeing inside the black box to understand why models do what they do.

HUT 4

CRYPTOGRAPHY

Securing model weights, training data and the infrastructure around them.

HUT 5

GOVERNANCE

Policy, standards and oversight, with a view from India and the Global South.

HUT 6

CONTAINMENT

Sandboxing, monitoring and safe deployment of autonomous agents.
