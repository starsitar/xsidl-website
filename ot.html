SECURING THE GRID’S CONTROL SYSTEMS

Inverters, battery storage, SCADA and the controllers behind them are now regulated security assets in India. Station Zero tests them on an isolated testbed, never on live plants, so OEMs, generators and investors can prove they are ready.

WHY NOW

Binding power-sector cyber rules from 1 April 2027

The CEA (Cyber Security in Power Sector) Regulations, 2026 replace voluntary guidelines with binding obligations across generation, transmission, distribution, load dispatch and storage, including generators, captive plants and storage above 50 MW.

OUR GROUND RULES
No active testing on live plants
We test on the lab testbed and replicas. On operating sites we use passive methods only.
Coordinated disclosure
Findings go to the OEM, and to CERT-In where appropriate, under an agreed process.
Compliance, not circumvention
We help vendors meet trusted-source rules, never work around them.
Sealed research data
Vulnerability data stays air-gapped and is never released publicly.
