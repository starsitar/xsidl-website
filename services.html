WHAT WE OFFER

Eight ways to test and prove your AI

01

AI AND AGENT RED TEAMING

We attack your chatbots, copilots and agents first: prompt injection, jailbreaks, data leakage, tool abuse and malicious MCP servers. Structured against OWASP LLM and Agentic Top 10, MITRE ATLAS and NIST AI RMF.

02

INDIC ADVERSARIAL TESTING

Global testing tools are built for English. We test against fraud and injection attacks in Hindi, Marathi and code-mixed language, from a growing attack library built in Indian finance.

03

THE SEALED TEST RANGE

An air-gapped environment in Mumbai where models are tested on sensitive or localised data that never leaves the room.

04

AIR-GAPPED AI REFERENCE LAB

A working on-prem AI stack you can walk into: local model, retrieval over confidential documents, logging and access controls. The reference architecture is published openly.

05

DEEPFAKE AND AI-FRAUD TESTBED

India-specific scenarios global vendors rarely cover: voice-cloned executive fraud, deepfaked video KYC and AI-scripted UPI scams.

06

AI ASSURANCE AND READINESS

Control checklists and evidence packs mapped to RBI FREE-AI, SEBI CSCRF, the DPDP Act and CERT-In. Includes readiness reviews for foreign AI vendors and fintechs entering India.

07

AI-ERA SOC AND AGENT MONITORING

Most SOCs can’t yet see what an AI agent is doing. We design monitoring for prompts, tool calls and agent actions, and test where AI-assisted triage helps and where it fails.

08

CONTINUOUS RE-TESTING

Models drift, get retrained and gain new tools. Periodic re-testing tracks whether defences still hold, with your test history kept as a running benchmark.
