WHY NOW

The build system is now part of the attack surface

Customers and regulators are starting to ask for proof of how software was built, not just a scan of the finished product. Meanwhile AI coding assistants and agents are writing and merging code faster than teams can review it.

POISONED DEPENDENCIES
Typosquatted, hijacked and confused packages ride straight into production builds.
COMPROMISED PIPELINES
CI runners, plug-ins and over-privileged tokens can alter code after review.
AI-WRITTEN CODE
Assistants and agents introduce code, packages and actions that need their own controls.
BILLS OF MATERIALS
CERT-In guidelines describe SBOMs and AI BOMs, and enterprise buyers increasingly ask for them.
EXPORT MARKETS
The EU Cyber Resilience Act’s vulnerability reporting duties have applied since September 2026.
PROOF, NOT PROMISES
Signed provenance lets anyone verify a release instead of trusting a questionnaire.

HOW AN ENGAGEMENT RUNS

Map, attack, harden, prove

STEP 1
MAP
Trace every path from a developer’s laptop to production: repos, runners, registries, secrets and people.
STEP 2
ATTACK
Red-team a replica of the pipeline in our sealed lab to find the shortest route to a tampered release.
STEP 3
HARDEN
Work with your engineers to close those routes, in your tools and without slowing delivery.
STEP 4
PROVE
Leave behind signed provenance, bills of materials and evidence your customers and auditors can check.
